Open World

Every street, building and shop here comes from OpenStreetMap. Walk up to a place to see what it is, or open the globe to go anywhere on Earth.

Open World

An open world of the whole planet, built from OpenStreetMap and other open data and explored on foot in the browser. Every street, building and shop is real; the details are generated. It started in Davis, California. The project is open source; contributions and ideas are welcome.
